We met in college, just two people who probably had no idea how important we would eventually become to each other. And somewhere between all the conversations, stupid jokes, fights, teasing, random moments, and countless memories, you became much more than just a friend. You became my sister. 🥹❤️ We weren't born into the same family. But somehow You became home. They say family is the people you're born with, But sometimes Family is the person who chooses to stay. I know I may not always say it perfectly, and I know I can be annoying, irritating, overprotective, or sometimes even stupid 😭, but beneath all of that is someone who genuinely feels incredibly lucky to have you in his life. You are simply great because, despite all the ups and downs in our relationship, you have always accepted me for who I am. Instead of forcing me to change myself, you have often tried to change yourself and meet me halfway. That is something I will always be grateful for. ❤️ Thank you for choosing to tie that Rakhi on my wrist. I don’t know what the future has planned for us, but I do know one thing: having a sister like you is a blessing I never knew I needed, and I am incredibly grateful that life gave me you. ❤️ Happy Rakshabandhan Vama 🥹🫂 Always your brother, Yash 🐷🐗
